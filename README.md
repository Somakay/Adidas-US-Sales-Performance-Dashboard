# Adidas-US-Sales-Performance-Dashboard
Power BI dashboard analyzing Adidas US sales and product performance
Power BI Portfolio Project | Adidas US Sales & Product Performance
In order to provide simple and interactive answers to executive and product-level business concerns, I recently created a three-page Power BI report that examined Adidas US sales and product performance.

🔍 Business Problem
How can decision‑makers:
Track overall performance in terms of sales and profitability
Recognize regional sales concentration in the United States. top-performing goods and merchants
Analyze the profitability of the product and its impact on overall sales.
Examine the trends of specific products throughout time.

# What the dashboards shows:
✅ Sales Performance Overview
Total Sales: $899.9M
Total Profit: $332.1M
Profit Margin: 37%
Clear sales acceleration in 2021
Geographic concentration across key U.S. states
Top products and retailers driving revenue

✅ Product Performance Deep Dive
Product contribution to total sales (treemap)
Profit margin comparison by product
Sales distribution by channel (In‑store, Outlet, Online)
Monthly sales trends for top‑performing products

Key Insights
Sales grew significantly in 2021 following a flatter 2020 period
Footwear products dominate total revenue contribution
Profitability varies meaningfully across products
In‑store sales remain the largest channel, with strong online contribution
